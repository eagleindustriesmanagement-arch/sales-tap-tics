# Competitive notes: AI sales role-play (October 2026)

These notes cover four AI role-play trainers: Second Nature, Hyperbound, Quantified and Yoodli. Vendor sites and help centers could not be fetched directly from this environment, so the research relied on web-search excerpts from vendor pages, help-center articles, press releases, and third-party reviews and comparisons. Everything comes from public sources, much of it vendor marketing, and some of it may be out of date. Claims we could not confirm are marked "reportedly."

## What each does well

### Second Nature
- Reps get a score and feedback 45 to 90 seconds after a session. The score is split into **knowledge** (content accuracy, topic coverage) and **style** (pace, clarity, energy, filler words), with a reported default weighting of 70/30. ([SN FAQ](https://secondnature.ai/faqs/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/229361/Second-Nature/))
- Sessions are short. Personalized role-plays are about 5 minutes and ready-made ones about 3 minutes, and reps can repeat them as often as they want. ([SN FAQ](https://secondnature.ai/faqs/))
- Admins can turn on a **video avatar** for any single-persona role-play. The avatar lip-syncs, blinks, nods and holds eye contact, and managers can reportedly assign a **mood** to a persona. ([SN video avatars](https://secondnature.ai/your-ai-role-play-got-more-real-with-video-avatars/), [SN vs Hyperbound](https://secondnature.ai/second-nature-vs-hyperbound-which-ai-sales-roleplay-platform-is-right-for-your-team/))
- Its gamified certification playbook enrolls every rep in a competition automatically. Each rep must complete the simulation at least once with an overall score of 80, and progress shows on dynamic leaderboards. ([Gamify Certification playbook PDF](https://go.secondnature.ai/hubfs/Gamify_Certification_Playbook_2023.pdf), [SN gamification](https://secondnature.ai/sales-training-gamification-a-new-approach-to-sales-skills-development/))
- An **AI Assistant** builds a role-play, including personas, context and objections, from a freeform description or an uploaded deck. Admins can then run a live preview and refine it. The product supports more than 20 languages, Spanish among them. ([SN Q2 2025 news](https://secondnature.ai/ai-role-play-training-news-q2-2025/))
- Managers reportedly get dashboards with completion rates and **proficiency heatmaps**, and can require a minimum score before reps take live calls. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/229361/Second-Nature/))

### Hyperbound
- The scorecard breaks results down criterion by criterion. Each criterion explains the score with **transcript citations** and suggests specific questions or talk tracks to try next time. ([Hyperbound Practice](https://www.hyperbound.ai/product/hyperbound-practice))
- Scoring is based on **concepts rather than keywords**. Asking "who typically makes purchases at your org?" counts as covering "decision maker." ([Hyperbound Practice](https://www.hyperbound.ai/product/hyperbound-practice))
- Difficulty ramps up in levels. Level 1 is a standard buyer, Level 2 adds follow-up objections and more skepticism, and Level 3 brings in multiple stakeholders. Reps can also build a **deal-specific persona** from the prospect's title, pains and likely objections. ([Objection-handling blog](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/ai-roleplays-objection-handling))
- Leaderboards rank reps on practice completion, scorecard performance and coaching engagement, not revenue. Hyperbound suggests running skill challenges such as "Best Objection Handling of the Week." ([Leaderboard software](https://www.hyperbound.ai/uses/sales-leaderboard-software), [Leaderboards blog](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/how-to-use-leaderboards-to-motivate-sales-practice))
- When a skill gap appears, the platform assigns a short targeted role-play, and an agent reportedly named "Kota" flags the pattern to the manager in Slack or Teams. ([Leaderboard software](https://www.hyperbound.ai/uses/sales-leaderboard-software))
- In the first-30-days walkthrough, the first bot and scorecard take under 10 minutes to build, while the full persona and module setup takes about two weeks. ([First 30 days](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/hyperbound-first-30-days-customer-walkthrough))

### Quantified
- Its **AI Compliance Agent** scores every practice session against approved messaging. Off-label terms cause an **instant fail**, and every session leaves audit-ready records. This is the closest match to our compliance engine. ([Compliance Agent](https://www.quantified.ai/platform/ai-compliance-agent), [Outdoo review](https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/quantified-review-and-pricing))
- Each session is scored against the customer's own approved criteria and **exemplars**, not a generic rubric. The vendor claims it covers 1,400+ behavioral dimensions in 40+ languages. ([AI Roleplay](https://www.quantified.ai/platform/ai-roleplay), [Search results](https://softwarefinder.com/sales-tools/quantified-ai))
- Its **readiness gates** replace completion tracking: a rep advances only after demonstrating the required behavior. The vendor says this shortens certification from 6 to 8 weeks down to 2 to 3. ([Readiness AI](https://www.quantified.ai/platform/readiness-ai), [Certification](https://www.quantified.ai/use-cases/certification-programs))
- **Field AI** gives reps a mobile pre-call brief with the goal, the approved message and the likely objections, plus a quick tailored role-play. After a visit, the rep talks through the call and the AI drafts a CRM note. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/quantified-announces-field-ai-to-capture-and-reveal-what-crms-miss-the-quality-of-field-execution-in-the-flow-of-work-302885662.html), [AI Field Coach](https://www.quantified.ai/platform/ai-field-coach))
- Some G2 reviewers reportedly say avatar responses can feel less dynamic or restrictive when the conversation goes off-script. The sample is small (about 16 reviews). ([Outdoo alternatives](https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/quantified-ai-alternatives))

### Yoodli
- **Chat-based role-plays** let reps type instead of speak. They can send multi-line messages, pause, and practice in a picture-in-picture window. Afterward they get a summary page with the full transcript, timestamps and coaching. ([Chat roleplays](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/15862405-create-chat-based-roleplays), [Release notes](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/9048809-yoodli-release-notes))
- Goals come in three types. **Rated** goals cover things like empathy and clarity, **binary** goals cover actions like confirming next steps, and **compound** goals combine related skills. Admins define what each score level means. ([Build roleplays](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/11565137-how-to-build-and-customize-roleplays))
- Learners can **practice again from the recording**. For assessments, admins can limit a role-play to a single attempt. ([Release notes](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/9048809-yoodli-release-notes))
- **Programs** are learning paths with release and due dates, assigned by group. They can set minimum passing scores before a rep is cleared for customer calls, and leaders get a dashboard of skill progression and certification status. ([Create a Program](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/11824812-how-to-create-a-program), [Sales roleplay](https://yoodli.ai/solutions/sales-roleplay), [Team Dashboard](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/10874726-team-dashboard))
- Coaching content reportedly appears at the relevant moment during a role-play. Separately, a meeting add-on shows private real-time nudges, such as a filler-word counter. ([Release notes](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/9048809-yoodli-release-notes), [Evro review](https://www.evro.ai/resources/yoodli-review-2026))

## The 10 ideas worth stealing

1. **Line-cited debrief** — Hyperbound explains each criterion's score with transcript citations and a suggested talk track. Apply: every behavior score, such as "isolated the real objection behind 'I need to talk to my wife'", links to the rep's exact line and shows a model line in English and in Miami Spanish. A compliance stop quotes the offending sentence, for example "this price is only good today."
2. **Redo from the moment** — Yoodli lets reps practice again from the recording, and Second Nature pushes reps to jump back in right away to raise their score. Apply: a "Redo this line" button rewinds the AI customer to just before the miss, so the rep retries only the trade-in or payment moment instead of the whole 5-minute session.
3. **Gap-triggered auto-assignment** — Hyperbound assigns a targeted short role-play when a skill gap appears and alerts the manager in Slack or Teams. Apply: a weak floor-check score on "payment too high" automatically queues a 3-minute drill on that objection, and the manager approves it with one tap from a phone notification instead of building assignments.
4. **Readiness gates, not completion** — Quantified advances reps only when they demonstrate the behavior. Apply: certification requires passing the core objection set in both English and Miami Spanish with zero compliance stops. The GM dashboard then shows who is cleared to work the floor alone.
5. **Hard-fail compliance with an audit trail** — Quantified instantly fails off-label language and keeps audit-ready records. Apply: we already stop sessions on made-up deadlines or invented facts. Add a store-level log the GM can export, recurring-violation trends per rep, and a debrief card explaining why the session stopped.
6. **Ramping difficulty and mood** — Hyperbound has Level 1 to 3 buyers, and Second Nature lets managers set persona moods. Apply: "talk to my wife" can start as Level 1 (polite stall), move to Level 2 (spouse on speakerphone with a follow-up objection), then Level 3 (spouse walks in mid-negotiation, multi-persona, possibly code-switching between English and Spanish).
7. **Short, repeatable sessions** — Second Nature's sessions run about 3 to 5 minutes and can be repeated without limit. Apply: build the core loop around a session of 3 minutes or less that fits between customers on the lot, so reps can practice several times a day.
8. **Pre-appointment brief and deal persona** — Quantified Field AI sends pre-call briefs with likely objections and a quick role-play, and Hyperbound builds deal-specific personas. Apply: before a booked appointment, the rep enters the customer's trade, credit situation, preferred language and stated concern, and gets a 2-minute warm-up against that customer.
9. **Skill challenges on the leaderboard** — Hyperbound runs weekly challenges like "Best Objection Handling," and Second Nature runs auto-enrolled certification competitions with a score of 80 to pass. Apply: a weekly store challenge on one objection, ranked by score rather than session count, shown on the GM dashboard (and on a showroom screen if the store opts in).
10. **Build scenarios from the store's own materials** — Second Nature's AI Assistant and Yoodli's Builder generate role-plays from uploaded content. Apply: the sales manager uploads this month's incentives and inventory sheet. The AI customer then asks about real units and real programs, and the compliance engine gets ground truth to check reps' claims against.

## Where Sales Taptics stands (October 3)

- **Already built:**
  - **4, readiness gates:** certification needs a pass, and stores can require it before taking ups.
  - **5, hard-fail compliance with an audit trail:** critical stop, the audit log, the compliance view and CSV
    export.
  - **6, ramping difficulty:** levels 1 to 3 per objection.
  - **7, short repeatable sessions:** turn-limited sessions and "Try again" on every debrief.
- **Applied in this pass:**
  - **1, line-cited debrief:** every scored behavior now names the behavior and quotes the rep's own line that
    earned or missed it, under the score.
  - **Honest score reveal** (the same idea): a partial offline score is no longer shown as "100 out of 100".
- **Next, in order of impact:**
  - **2, redo from the moment:** the session can already be rebuilt from stored turns, which is the base for
    rewinding to the missed line.
  - **3, gap-triggered drills** that the manager approves.
  - **8, a pre-appointment warm-up** against the customer the rep is about to meet.
  - **9, a weekly store challenge:** ranked on one objection, opt-in, kept off the reps' own screens so it does not
    discourage practice (spec 14.6).
  - **10, scenarios from the store's own inventory and incentives.**
